# County Brand Hub - Strategic Implementation Guide

## Executive Summary

Based on Spencer Pratt's successful viral GOP messaging strategy and proven county-level success models (Hopkins County, Montgomery County), here's how to build an unstoppable county-level brand platform within GA Voter Action.

---

## The Spencer Pratt Lesson: Why This Works

### What Spencer Pratt Got Right
1. **Visual storytelling over text** - AI videos, memes, dramatic imagery
2. **Made it shareable** - Easy one-click amplification across social media
3. **Local hero narrative** - Showed regular people doing extraordinary things
4. **Competitive energy** - Counties comparing, competing, celebrating
5. **Authentic grassroots feel** - User-generated, not top-down corporate
6. **Movement building** - Not just politics, but a lifestyle/community

### What GA Voter Action Can Do Better
Spencer Pratt's approach was about entertainment. Your approach should be about **real community impact** - making it undeniable and quantifiable.

## Content Strategy: What to Feature

### Daily/Weekly Content Pillars

**MONDAY: Leadership Spotlight**
- Feature 1 county leader per day
- Bio + photo + top accomplishment
- "Meet [Name], [County] leader making a difference"
- Community encourages engagement

**TUESDAY: Success Story**
- Feature best volunteer story from previous week
- Photo + testimonial
- "Real results from real people in [County]"
- Push to social media

**WEDNESDAY: County Stats**
- 3-5 counties with highest growth that week
- Real metrics: volunteer hours, activities, impact scores
- "These counties are crushing it"
- Competitive/celebratory tone

**THURSDAY: Leader Accomplishment**
- Highlight a recent pass bill/initiative/win
- Photo + impact statement
- "Vote for this - see what happens"
- Call to action to volunteer

**FRIDAY: Weekly Roundup**
- Top 5 stories of the week
- Top 5 leaders of the week
- Top 5 counties of the week
- Email digest to all volunteers

**SUNDAY: Upcoming Events**
- Promote next week's town halls, events, opportunities
- Make it easy for volunteers to participate
- "Get involved this week"

## Success Stories Format Guide

### What Makes a Great Story (Examples)

#### Example 1: Quantified Impact
**Title:** "We Registered 47 New Voters in [County] This Month"
**Photo:** Group of volunteers with new voter registration forms
**Story:** "Started canvassing in March with no prior experience. By May, our team had talked to 423 people and registered 47 new voters. Each conversation was a chance to share why voting matters. This is what making a difference looks like."
**Impact:** 47 new voters registered
**Points:** 100

#### Example 2: Leadership Connection
**Title:** "County Commissioner Jones Listened to Us"
**Photo:** Volunteer with Commissioner at town hall
**Story:** "Went to town hall nervous about speaking up. Commissioner Jones not only listened but acted. Within 6 weeks, the pothole-filled Main Street got fixed. Proof that our voices matter and our leaders care."
**Impact:** Direct government response to constituent concern
**Points:** 75

#### Example 3: Youth Engagement
**Title:** "Got My 18-Year-Old Daughter to Vote"
**Photo:** Mother and daughter with voting stickers
**Story:** "She thought voting didn't matter. Explained why I care about the future. She went to the polls for the first time. Seeing her exercise her right to vote was the proudest moment of my life as a parent."
**Impact:** New first-time voter
**Points:** 150

#### Example 4: Community Service
**Title:** "30 Volunteers Cleaned Up Downtown"
**Photo:** Before/after of cleaned-up park
**Story:** "Organized a cleanup and was amazed at turnout. 30 people showed up. We're not just volunteers - we're neighbors caring for our community. People asked what we do next. That's the power of showing up."
**Impact:** 30 volunteers engaged, visible community improvement
**Points:** 200

## Success Metrics & KPIs

### Engagement Metrics
- **Story Upload Rate:** % of counties posting stories (target: 60% within 3 months)
- **Stories Per Month:** Average stories per county (target: 15/month)
- **Story Reach:** Views per story (target: 100+ avg)
- **Social Shares:** % of stories shared externally (target: 25%)
- **Comments:** Engagement on stories (target: 3+ per story avg)

### Content Metrics
- **Leader Profiles:** % of local officials with profiles (target: 80% of key leaders)
- **Accomplishment Posts:** New accomplishments per month (target: 20+)
- **Asset Downloads:** Downloads of brand assets (target: 500+/month)
- **Asset Usage:** How often used externally (survey)

### Community Metrics
- **Active Counties:** # counties with at least 1 story (target: 80+ by month 3)
- **Volunteer Retention:** Months until volunteer becomes inactive (target: 6+ months)
- **New Volunteer Recruitment:** Attribution of new volunteers to brand hub (target: 15%)

### Business Metrics
- **Time Savings:** Hours saved by using templated assets vs creating from scratch (target: 80 hrs/month saved)
- **Media Coverage:** Local news stories mentioning county leaders from GA Voter Action (target: 5+/month)
- **Donation Lift:** Correlation between featured story and campaign donations (survey/test)

## Risk Mitigation

### Content Moderation
- ✅ All leader profiles must use verified public information only
- ✅ All accomplishments must be from public record
- ✅ Story flagging system for inappropriate content
- ✅ Community moderation by county coordinators
- ✅ Automated profanity filter

### Brand Safety
- ✅ Clear separation: factual accomplishments vs opinions
- ✅ No attack content on opponents
- ✅ No misleading statistics
- ✅ Third-party fact checking for major claims
- ✅ Legal review for all leader content

### Competitive Health
- ✅ Rankings focus on growth, not absolute numbers (incentivizes all counties)
- ✅ No shaming - celebrate all effort
- ✅ Multiple ways to win (stories, stats, engagement)
- ✅ Feature small counties and grassroots just as much as large ones
